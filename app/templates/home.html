Why Timely Vaccination Matters

Vaccines are scientifically formulated to build immunity during critical early development windows. Administering doses on time protects against life-threatening diseases including Tuberculosis, Polio, Hepatitis B, Rotavirus, Diphtheria, and Measles.

How VaxiCare Works

A clear, reliable workflow connecting parents with community healthcare workers.

1. Register Child Profile

Add your child's date of birth and village. VaxiCare immediately generates an individualized 19-dose immunization schedule based on age milestones.

2. Receive Timely Reminders

Get proactive alerts before each dose becomes due via in-app alerts, browser push notifications, and optional SMS so you never miss an appointment.

3. Verified Administration

Authorized healthcare workers record administered vaccines at the health centre. Your digital record updates instantly with downloadable PDF and QR verification.

Data Protection

Committed to Health Data Privacy & Security

VaxiCare strictly implements role-based access control (RBAC), CSRF protection, secure salted password hashing, and session hardening. Parents retain full control over their contact preferences and SMS reminders, while clinical records remain auditable and tamper-evident.
