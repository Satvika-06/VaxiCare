About VaxiCare

VaxiCare is a bilingual, community-oriented immunization and health tracking system designed for families, frontline health workers and administrators. It simplifies the management of child vaccination data while encouraging timely follow-up for vaccines due today, upcoming or overdue.

Our goal is to improve public health awareness and promote stronger community-level vaccination coverage without replacing medical advice from authorized professionals. The reference schedule in the system is based on the Government of India UIP National Immunization Schedule and must be confirmed by a qualified healthcare provider.

Key principles

  • Secure child records
  • Parent-friendly reminders
  • Health worker support
  • Informational schedule only
